# OGP Interview Rubric Dataset

## Round 1: 45-minute PM Interview

### Rubric Items

1. **Ability to handle ambitious projects**
   - Scale of the problem
   - Resource constraints
   - Complexity of the solution
   - Outcome achieved

2. **Problem-solving + entrepreneurial spirit**
   - Approach to problem identification
   - Creativity of solutions
   - Risk-taking
   - Iteration and learning

3. **Technical expertise + understanding of dev constraints**
   - Technical knowledge relevant to the role
   - Ability to communicate with engineers
   - Understanding of technical trade-offs
   - Experience with shipping technical products

4. **Motivation for SG public sector**
   - Alignment with public good mission
   - Understanding of Singapore's public sector challenges
   - Specific examples of public service orientation
   - Why OGP specifically

### Cultural Values (OGP's Wording)

1. **VC operating model**
   - Product officers own decisions, not bosses
   - Autonomy to experiment and iterate
   - Focus on outcomes over process

2. **Operate in highly ambiguous space**
   - Graduate-student vs undergrad analogy (explore, test hypotheses)
   - Comfort with uncertainty
   - Ability to pivot based on learning

3. **Officer autonomy + responsibility**
   - "Slime mould" strategy (decentralized decision-making)
   - Taking ownership of end-to-end outcomes
   - Accountability for results

4. **Impact = public good**
   - Citizen experience
   - Man-hours saved
   - Cost avoidance
   - Not market share or revenue

5. **Bias for action**
   - "Do what it takes" attitude
   - Willingness to cross functions (engineering, design, legal, marketing)
   - Focus on getting things done quickly

### Interview Structure

- **Round 1**: 45-minute PM interview (rubric-based)
- **Round 2a**: 7-day take-home product write-up
- **Round 2b**: 60-minute hiring manager on-site
- **Round 3**: 45-minute Head of Product on-site

Beeready (voice AI interview coach for Indonesian scholarship candidates) maps to all OGP values:

1. **VC operating model**: Solo PM + builder, owned all decisions
2. **Ambiguous space**: No prior voice AI or rubric-based scoring experience
3. **Autonomy/responsibility**: End-to-end ownership from idea to launch
4. **Public good impact**: Leveling the playing field for underprivileged candidates
5. **Bias for action**: Shipped v1 in 6 weeks
